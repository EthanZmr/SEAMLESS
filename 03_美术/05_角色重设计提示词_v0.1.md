# 阿针 / 阿银 重设计提示词 v0.1

> 用途：给图像模型重新出角色概念图，或在你现有两张图上做局部改图。
> 重要假设：**阿银按“白城量体师 / 裁断者系统原型”写，不是阿绫的实体化。**
> 如果阿银其实就是阿绫，请直接跳到第 5 节，不要给她实体和剪刀。

---

## 0. 先定三件事，提示词才有意义

1. **用途**：这是**立绘/对话头像**，还是**游戏内 96px sprite 参考**？
   - 立绘：可以保留细节、chibi、复杂材质。
   - 游戏内：必须简化成 14 件切件，96px 可读；不能直接缩图。
2. **比例**：你原来的图是约 3 头身 chibi。
   - 继续 chibi：适合头像/图鉴，不适合 27 岁地下诊所的 M 级叙事，也不适合 96px 平台跳跃。
   - 推荐：立绘用 **6.5–7 头身**，游戏内用简化 cutout；chibi 只做可选头像。
3. **阿银是谁**：
   - 推荐：白城的“量体师/裁断者原型”，阿针的镜像对手；不是双胞胎，不是换色。
   - 不推荐：阿绫实体化。阿绫没有身体；给她身体会拆掉故事核心。

下面所有 prompt 都按 **6.5 头身、成年、非 chibi** 写；如果你要 chibi，用第 4 节的 chibi 修饰词替换比例描述。

## 1. 阿针 · 主立绘 Prompt（英文，可直接复制）

Wiry, compact, 1.70 m, slightly forward-leaning posture, tired and guarded, androgynous-coded but clearly adult. Short black undercut hair, shaved left side, a few dark metal hairpins, old scar through the left eyebrow, dark circles, calm annoyed expression, no makeup. She wears a short high-collar dark blue work jacket with the left sleeve removed, rolled right sleeve, oil stains and 5–7 mismatched fabric patches in rust red, amber and teal; a simple tool harness instead of a corset; heavy worn boots; a single red surgical thread stitched from her left collarbone to her left shoulder, clearly visible. Her LEFT arm (image right) is an exposed second-hand industrial prosthetic forearm, slightly thicker than the right arm, matte gunmetal, visible cables at the elbow, a thin cyan seam light around the wrist. Her right hand wears a fingerless work glove and holds one long steel sewing needle in reverse grip, needle about 25 cm, cyan thread running from the needle eye to a small spool. Exactly two thread spools clipped to her right hip (image left): one cyan, one rust red. Strong readable silhouette: asymmetric left arm, short jacket, two hip spools, red shoulder thread. 3/4 full body, knees slightly bent, weight on the back foot, needle held low like a tool, not a sword; looking at the viewer with tired annoyance. Rain-market alley at night behind her, out-of-focus neon signs, wet asphalt reflections, dark teal and magenta, cold cyan key light from the left, warm amber bounce from a food stall, wet fabric specular. Stylized 2D game character concept art, clean shape language, painterly pixel-art hybrid, strong silhouette, mature proportions, no photorealistic rendering, no text, no watermark, no logo. --ar 1:1 --style raw

## 3. 改图 Prompt（在你现有两张图上局部改，不重画脸）

> 用法：把原图作为 image reference / 局部重绘底图；先锁脸、锁姿势，只改服装和武器。
> 如果模型支持 inpainting，先 mask 掉要改的区域。

### 3.1 阿针 改图

```text
Image-to-image edit of the provided character. Preserve the face, eyes, hairstyle silhouette, pose and camera exactly. Change only the costume and props: remove the fur collar, corset, ornate gold buckles and excessive jewelry; replace with a short high-collar dark blue work jacket, rolled right sleeve, no left sleeve, oil stains and 5–7 small rust/amber/teal fabric patches, a simple tool harness and heavy worn boots. Add an exposed second-hand industrial prosthetic LEFT forearm (image right), slightly thicker than the right arm, matte gunmetal with visible elbow cables and a thin cyan seam light at the wrist. Add one red surgical thread stitched from the left collarbone to the left shoulder, clearly visible. Replace the large decorative spool with exactly two small thread spools clipped to the right hip (image left), one cyan and one rust red. Keep the sewing needle but make it thicker, 25 cm, reverse grip, cyan thread from needle eye to spool. Reduce gold to small tool accents only; overall palette dark blue, rust, amber, cyan, one red accent. Keep the rain-market background but push it out of focus. Do not change the face, do not add text, do not add extra fingers or limbs. --ar 1:1 --style raw
```

## 4. 96px 游戏内参考 Prompt（不是直接可用的 sprite）

> 图像模型做不出严格可切片的 14 件套；这个 prompt 只用于**三视图/剪影参考**。正式切件仍需在 PS/Krita/Blender 里做。

```text
Character turnaround reference sheet, same character, three views: front, left side, back, orthographic, neutral standing pose, arms slightly away from body, full body, feet visible, flat cel colors, minimal shading, no background, no text. A-Zhen: 27-year-old female underground repairer, short black undercut, left eyebrow scar, short dark blue work jacket, exposed left prosthetic forearm, two thread spools on right hip, one red shoulder thread, long steel needle in right hand. Strong readable silhouette at 96 px tall, exaggerated key shapes, no tiny details. Clean 2D game art, flat color blocks, 2 px dark outline, limited palette: dark blue, charcoal, rust, amber, cyan, one red accent. --ar 3:2 --style raw
```

阿银版把主体替换为：

```text
A-Yin: 32-year-old white-city measurement officer, tall, long silver high ponytail, ivory seamless long coat with no visible stitching, one cyan brand pin, graphite under-suit, elegant long tailor's shears, white measuring tape with cyan laser line, no red. Strong vertical silhouette, distinct from A-Zhen, 96 px readable.
```

### 96px 验收

- 纯黑、纯白、雨市霓虹三种背景各看一次。
- 96px 还能认出：阿针 = 左义体 + 两线轴 + 肩红线；阿银 = 高马尾 + 无缝合白大衣 + 长剪/软尺。
- 48px 不要求认细节，只要求“黑/白两个人”不混。
- 手、针、剪刀是模型最容易画错的地方；出图后必须放大检查手指和工具结构。

## 5. 如果阿银其实是阿绫（替代方案）

**不要用上面的实体阿银 prompt。** 阿绫没有身体；她是阿针脑内的意识、故障 overlay。

```text
Holographic glitch overlay of A-Ling, a 17-year-old girl's silhouette, no physical body, translucent, 2–4 px chromatic aberration, magenta and cyan scanlines, pixel noise, white-hot edges at high sync, calm or teasing expression, faint outline only, never blocking the center of the screen. No scissors, no coat, no boots, no solid body. She appears behind A-Zhen's shoulder or at the screen edge like a signal, not a ghost. Transparent background, game VFX concept art, no text, no watermark. --ar 1:1
```

如果阿银是**白城版阿针/克隆体**，保留白发白大衣，但必须回答：她为什么有身体？她是不是白露的“无缝”实验品？否则玩家会把她当成阿针的换色皮。

## 6. 出图后的验收清单

- [ ] 阿针左前臂是义体，不是两只人手。
- [ ] 左肩红线存在，且不是普通装饰线。
- [ ] 右腰正好两个线轴，不是一堆挂件。
- [ ] 针是工具尺寸，不是长矛/法杖。
- [ ] 没有毛领/束胸/大面积金饰/高跟战斗靴。
- [ ] 阿银没有可见缝线（除非剧情故意）。
- [ ] 阿银没有红色；红只属于旧城/危险/接缝。
- [ ] 阿银不是阿针的镜像姿势/同脸换色。
- [ ] 两人站在一起，剪影能区分：阿针“短、横、工具”，阿银“长、直、量具”。
- [ ] 96px 缩图后仍能认出关键识别点。
- [ ] 手指、针、剪刀结构正确；没有多余肢体。
- [ ] 记录制作方式（手绘/AI/混合）、模型、提示词、种子，用于资产登记。

## 7. 模型使用建议

| 工具 | 建议 |
|---|---|
| Midjourney | 用 `--ar 1:1 --style raw`；把原图作为 image prompt，`--iw 1.5–2` 锁脸；负向词用 `--no`。 |
| SDXL / ComfyUI | 1024×1024；正面/负面分开；用 ControlNet OpenPose 锁姿势，IP-Adapter 锁脸；先出 4 张选一张，再做 inpaint。 |
| Nano Banana / Gemini 图像 | 用自然语言“保持脸和姿势，只改服装/武器”；一次只改一类，别一次改太多。 |
| 局部改图 | 先 mask 衣服/手臂/武器，不要整图重绘；脸和手最后修。 |

## 8. 一句话总结

**阿针的 prompt 关键词是“劳动、左义体、红线、两个线轴、工具针”；阿银的关键词是“白城、无缝合、长剪、软尺、量你”。**

先把这两个识别系统画对，再谈 chibi、像素密度和衣服褶子。
